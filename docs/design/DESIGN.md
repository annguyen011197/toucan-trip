# Fidelity Design System

This document outlines the core aesthetic and functional principles of the Fidelity Design System. It is built upon a foundation of clarity, professionalism, and user-centric design, ensuring a consistent and engaging experience across all applications.

## Brand Identity

Our brand identity is defined by a modern, approachable aesthetic, leveraging a sophisticated color palette and clean typography.

### Color Palette

The Fidelity Design System utilizes a carefully curated color palette to establish hierarchy, convey meaning, and enhance visual appeal.

*   **Primary Color**: `#057be8` - This vibrant blue serves as the cornerstone of our brand, reflecting trustworthiness and innovation. It's used for primary actions, critical information, and key brand elements.
*   **Secondary Color**: `#5e78a3` - A muted, supportive blue-grey, this color complements the primary blue, providing a gentle contrast for secondary actions, subtle distinctions, and less prominent UI elements.
*   **Tertiary Color**: `#ce6000` - A warm, engaging orange accent, this color is reserved for highlights, badges, and elements requiring a distinct pop of attention without overwhelming the primary experience.
*   **Neutral Color**: `#74777f` - A balanced grey, this forms the basis for backgrounds, surfaces, and typography, ensuring readability and visual comfort.

### Typography

Our typography system is designed for optimal readability and a clear information hierarchy. We primarily use the `Work Sans` typeface across all elements.

*   **Headline Font**: `Work Sans` - Used for all major headings and titles, ensuring strong visual impact and clear division of content.
*   **Body Font**: `Work Sans` - Applied to all paragraph text and general content, prioritizing legibility and a comfortable reading experience.
*   **Label Font**: `Work Sans` - Employed for labels, input fields, and other interactive elements, maintaining consistency and clarity throughout the UI.

## Visual Attributes

Beyond color and typography, specific visual attributes define the tactile feel and overall user experience of our interface.

### Roundedness

Our UI elements feature a **moderate roundedness (2)**. This provides a soft, friendly aesthetic while maintaining a professional and structured appearance. It avoids overly sharp edges or extreme pill shapes, striking a balance that is both modern and accessible.

### Spacing

The design system employs **normal spacing (2)**. This ensures a comfortable amount of whitespace within and between UI elements, promoting readability, reducing cognitive load, and creating a balanced visual layout without feeling too dense or overly spacious. It allows elements to breathe while efficiently utilizing screen real estate.
